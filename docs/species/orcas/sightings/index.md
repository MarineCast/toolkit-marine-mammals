## Understand and use the outputs

| Guide | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| [Sources and coverage](sources.md) | Provider capabilities, missing coverage, and source-specific rights |
| [Processing and imputation](methods.md) | Normalization, regional support, binary ecotype inference, and scientific gates |
| [Releases and reports](products.md) | Query artifacts, immutable generations, consumer pointers, and retention |

Sightings are reported records, not abundance, survey effort, or verified
absence. An imputed observation label remains separate from a direct source
label and from modeled occurrence. Annual population values belong in the
[Census area](../census/index.md).
